PhD Thesis: Physical and Projected Pairs of Quasars
(supervised by A. Treves).
I investigated the cool gas and the dark matter surrounding quasars using optical spectroscopy of physical and projected quasar pairs.

MSc Thesis: Globular Cluster Systems in Virgo Cluster's Dwarf Galaxies
(supervised by M. Colpi, G. Gavazzi, and L. Giordano).
I used multiwavelength HST and SDSS imaging to study the color distributions of globular clusters (GCs) and nuclear star clusters (NSCs) in dwarf galaxies of the Virgo Cluster. I probed the effects of the cluster environment on galaxies by comparing the properties of GCs and NSCs with those of the average galactic stellar population.

BSc Thesis: Gravitational encounters of BH binaries in globular clusters
(supervised by M. Colpi and B. Devecchi).
I simulated the dynamical effects of gravitational encounters between binary black holes on the early evolution of globular clusters.
