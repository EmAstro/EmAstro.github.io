Curriculum Vitæ

In Summary

I am an observational astronomer, and since October 2021 I have been an associate scientist at Gemini Observatory. Previously, I was an Archive Science Engineer in ESO's Archive Science group (outsourced from Terma GmbH). I have also been a visitor at the Max-Planck-Institut für Astrophysik in Garching bei München, and a postdoctoral researcher at the University of California in Santa Barbara and at the Max-Planck-Institut für Astronomie in Heidelberg. I did my PhD at the Università degli Studi dell'Insubria and my undergraduate studies at the Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca (including a few months at the Universität Zürich).

My research focuses mainly on quasars and blazars, from the local Universe up to the end of the cosmic dark ages at z≈7. In particular, I am interested in their clustering properties and in their circum-galactic medium.

I am an instrument scientist for GNIRS at Gemini, and I took part in the commissioning of the GNIRS IFUs and of IGRINS-2.
