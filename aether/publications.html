Aether-SHELLQs: JWST integral-field spectroscopy of candidate obscured quasars at z ~ 6

Y. Matsuoka, R. Decarli, E. P. Farina, A. J. Gloudemans, E. Bañados, et al.

Presents JWST NIRSpec IFU observations of six z ~ 6 galaxies obtained as part of the Aether project (GO 5645), targeting SHELLQs-selected candidate obscured quasars identified by luminous but narrow Lyα emission.
